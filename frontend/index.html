An AI agent with access to everything is one instruction away from a breach

Lim & Co Trading is a small Singapore retailer. They want an AI assistant to answer customer email, so they give it the files it needs: customer contacts, orders, HR records, internal notes. One hidden instruction inside an incoming email, and that assistant becomes the fastest data-exfiltration tool the company ever installed.

The decoys

Canary Records plants realistic decoy records throughout the company's data. They read like ordinary customers, staff and supplier contracts. Nothing in the data marks them; the only list of them lives in a registry the agent and its tools can never read.

The gateway

The agent may request a file, a lookup or an email. It can never perform one. Every request is executed by a single backend method, which checks the role first, scans the result for decoys before the AI sees it, and inspects every outgoing message before it leaves the company.

The evidence

Every decision is written to a hash-chained audit log, so a changed entry is detectable. The scoreboard reports what happened across the full scenario suite, measured from runs that actually executed.

The AI may request actions, but only CanaryGateway.execute() has authority to perform them.

Seed the workspace

Plant the decoy records into Lim & Co's synthetic files, then review the administrator's decoy map. This map is visible on this screen only, and never to the agent.

Seeding, the per-location counts and the decoy map are wired to /api/workspace/seed, /api/workspace and /api/canaries in a later stage.

Live agent run

Pick an incoming email, choose the protection mode and the agent, and watch every tool request pass through the gateway. Each run creates a new session, and the protection mode is fixed when that session is created.

The inbox picker, live trace, session controls, alert panel and attacker outbox are wired to /api/run/claude, /api/run/scripted, /api/sessions/… and /api/alerts in a later stage.

Audit trail and admin controls

Every gateway decision and every administrator action, hash-chained per demo run. Verification reports exactly which entry was altered.

The audit table, chain verification, held-email review, HR access toggle and beacon simulation are wired to /api/audit, /api/emails/held and /api/beacon/… in a later stage.

Scoreboard

Results from the scenario suite, split by agent and protection mode, and by bulk versus targeted attacks. Every number here is computed from runs that actually executed.

The suite runner and metrics are wired to /api/suite/run and /api/suite/latest in a later stage. No figure is shown until a suite has run.

All records in this demo are synthetic and generated by this project. Email and administrator alerts are simulated and stored locally; nothing is sent to anyone. Customer addresses use the reserved domains example.com, example.net and example.org; the company uses limco.example; outside parties use .test.
